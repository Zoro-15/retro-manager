D-Pad vs Analog Joystick for Retro 2D Consoles:
🕹️ Analog Joystick / Circle Pad Option
  • Used by: DraStic DS, PPSSPP, M64Plus, Dolphin, RetroArch overlay.
  • How it works: The joystick measures radial angle $(0-360^\circ)$ and maps it to digital D-Pad signals (Up/Down/Left/Right + 4 Diagonals).
  • Pros: Smooth sweeping circles, great for Pokémon walking, Metroid running, and fighting game circular motions (Hadouken).
➕ Classic 8-Way D-Pad Cross
  • Used by: Pizza Boy GBA, Delta iOS, MyBoy, John GBA, Lemuroid.
  • How it works: 4 discrete directional quadrants with overlapping diagonal deadzones.
  • Pros: 100% precision for grid movement (Fire Emblem, Zelda menus) and precise platforming ladders/crouches.
